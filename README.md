# 📖 Overview

Netflix has transformed the entertainment industry by becoming one of the world's largest streaming platforms. This project performs an in-depth analysis of the Netflix Titles Dataset to understand how the platform has evolved over time.

Using Python and powerful data analysis libraries, this project extracts meaningful insights from thousands of movies and TV shows available on Netflix.

The analysis focuses on:

- 📺 Content Distribution
- 🌍 Country-wise Production
- 🎭 Genre Popularity
- ⭐ Rating Distribution
- 📈 Platform Growth
- ⏳ Duration Trends
- 🎬 Release vs Added Patterns

---

# 🎯 Objectives

- Clean and preprocess real-world Netflix data.
- Analyze Movies and TV Shows separately.
- Discover global content trends.
- Understand Netflix's expansion strategy.
- Visualize data using professional charts.
- Generate actionable business insights.

# 📈 Exploratory Data Analysis

## 1️⃣ Movies vs TV Shows

Netflix is primarily a movie platform.

| Content Type | Percentage |
|--------------|-----------|
| 🎬 Movies | **70.4%** |
| 📺 TV Shows | **29.6%** |

**Insight**

- Movies dominate the platform.
- TV Shows represent a smaller but steadily growing share.

---

## 2️⃣ Content Added Over Time

One of the biggest findings is Netflix's explosive growth after **2015**.

### Peak Growth

| Year | Titles Added |
|------|--------------|
| 2017 | 1163 |
| 2018 | 1625 |
| 2019 | **1999** |
| 2020 | 1878 |
| 2021 | 1498 |

### Insight

- Slow growth before 2015.
- Massive expansion from 2016 onwards.
- 2019 marked the highest content addition.

---

## 3️⃣ Top Content Producing Countries

Top contributors include:

🥇 United States

🥈 India

🥉 United Kingdom

Followed by:

- Canada
- France
- Japan
- Spain
- South Korea

### Insight

Netflix has expanded far beyond Hollywood and increasingly invests in international productions.

## 4️⃣ Most Popular Genres

Top genres include:

- 🌍 International Movies
- 🎭 Dramas
- 😂 Comedies
- 📺 International TV Shows
- 📚 Documentaries
- 💥 Action & Adventure
- 🎬 Independent Movies
- ❤️ Romantic Movies

### Insight

International content has become Netflix's strongest category, reflecting its global audience strategy.

---

## 5️⃣ Rating Distribution

Most common ratings:

- TV-MA
- TV-14
- TV-PG
- R
- PG-13

### Insight

Netflix primarily targets teenage and adult audiences with mature content.

---

## 6️⃣ Movie Duration Analysis

Observations:

- Older films generally had longer runtimes.
- Modern movies mostly fall between **90–120 minutes**.
- Some significant outliers exist.

---

## 7️⃣ TV Show Duration

Findings:

- Many shows consist of very short episodes.
- Short-form content has increased in recent years.
- Netflix continues experimenting with bite-sized entertainment.

---

## 8️⃣ Genre Trends Over Time

Key observations:

- Dramas consistently remain popular.
- Comedy maintains strong growth.
- International content has grown rapidly.
- Overall genre diversity has expanded significantly.

---

## 9️⃣ Release Year vs Date Added

Interesting observation:

Many classic movies released decades ago were only recently added to Netflix.

This demonstrates Netflix's strategy of balancing:

- Classic titles
- Recent releases
- Original productions
